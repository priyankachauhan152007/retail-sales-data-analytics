# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Findings

## 1. Introduction

Exploratory Data Analysis was performed on the retail sales dataset using Python and Pandas. The objective was to understand overall sales performance, product category performance, customer purchasing patterns, and monthly sales trends.

## 2. Dataset Overview

* Total transactions: 1,000
* Total original columns: 9
* Total recorded revenue: ₹4,56,000
* Average transaction amount: ₹456
* Highest transaction amount: ₹2,000
* Lowest transaction amount: ₹25

## 3. Product Category Analysis

Electronics generated the highest revenue of ₹1,56,905, followed by Clothing at ₹1,55,580 and Beauty at ₹1,43,515.

Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold, with 894 units. Electronics sold 849 units, while Beauty sold 771 units.

These findings show that the category generating the highest revenue is not necessarily the category with the highest quantity sold.

## 4. Gender-Based Sales Analysis

Female customers contributed ₹2,32,840 in sales, representing approximately 51.1% of total revenue.

Male customers contributed ₹2,23,160, representing approximately 48.9% of total revenue.

The difference between the two groups is relatively small.

## 5. Monthly Sales Analysis

May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales at ₹53,150.

October 2023 recorded ₹46,580, while December 2023 recorded ₹44,690.

September 2023 had the lowest sales among the reported months in 2023, at ₹23,620.

January 2024 recorded ₹1,530. This month requires further investigation because the dataset may contain only partial-month data.

## 6. Key Findings

1. Total recorded sales amounted to ₹4,56,000.
2. Electronics generated the highest revenue.
3. Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold.
4. Female customers contributed approximately 51.1% of total revenue.
5. May 2023 was the strongest month by recorded sales.
6. January 2024 requires additional checking before meaningful monthly comparisons can be made.

## 7. Conclusion

The initial EDA identified differences in revenue across product categories, purchasing quantities, customer groups, and months.

These findings provide a foundation for further analysis and visualizations. The next stage will use charts to make the results easier to understand and communicate.

The findings describe patterns in this dataset and do not, by themselves, establish the causes of those patterns.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Electronics Inventory Planning:** Monitor popular electronic products and maintain sufficient stock to support demand.

2. **Increase Clothing Revenue:** Explore product bundles, seasonal promotions, and discounts to improve clothing sales.

3. **Use Inclusive Marketing:** Since sales are nearly evenly distributed by gender, develop campaigns that appeal to both male and female customers.

4. **Investigate Monthly Trends:** Examine possible reasons for lower sales in September 2023 and identify opportunities to improve performance during slower months.

## Conclusion

The dataset contains 1,000 retail transactions with total sales of ₹456,000. Electronics generated the highest revenue, while Clothing had the highest quantity sold. Sales were distributed almost equally between male and female customers. These findings can support inventory planning, marketing decisions, and sales monitoring.
